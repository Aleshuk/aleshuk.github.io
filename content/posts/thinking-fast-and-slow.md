**Thinking, Fast and Slow** is one of those rare books that quietly changes how you watch your own mind work.

## The big idea

Kahneman describes two systems of thought:

- **System 1** — fast, automatic, intuitive. Great for catching a ball, terrible for statistics.
- **System 2** — slow, deliberate, effortful. Where careful reasoning lives, but it's lazy and easily tired.

Most of our errors come from letting System 1 answer questions that really needed System 2.

## Why it stuck with me

Working with data, I see this every day: a chart *feels* like it says one thing, and it takes real effort to check whether it actually does. The book is a good reminder to slow down before trusting a first impression.

> Nothing in life is as important as you think it is while you are thinking about it.

## Verdict

★★★★★ — Dense in places, but worth it. A book to re-read, not just read.
